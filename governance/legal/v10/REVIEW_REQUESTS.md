5. Are any cultural or spiritual activities subject to specific organizational registration, and where?
6. Does a particular cross-border contract, hosting arrangement or data flow create an actual local connection?
7. Which supporting and contrary authorities, dates, statutory exceptions, remedies and procedural rules apply?
