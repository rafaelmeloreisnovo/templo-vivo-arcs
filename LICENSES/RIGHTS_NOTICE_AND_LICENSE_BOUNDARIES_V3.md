## 1. Operative hierarchy; non-retroactivity
1. **Applicable mandatory law, statutory exceptions and independently held rights** prevail where relevant.
2. Valid express **third-party and upstream grants**, contractual obligations, employment arrangements, contributor rights, and earlier licenses retain their own legal effect. A repository owner cannot relicense what the owner does not own; a later notice does not automatically withdraw earlier grants.
3. Where genuinely and lawfully applicable, an **asset-specific express license** controls its defined grant, subject to law.
4. `LICENSE-RAFCODE-PROPRIETARY-V1.md` applies **only** to assets within its own lawful scope; this V3 notice does not enlarge that scope or adopt a new grant.
5. `LICENSE_POLICY.md` is the repository's current policy layer. `LICENSES/SOFTWARE_LICENSE_DECISION.md` records **TOKEN_VAZIO / DECISION_REQUIRED** for a repository-wide standard software-license selection.
6. Root `LICENSE` is a **historical manifesto, not a recognized SPDX license**; preserve its byte history for provenance. Historical claims in it are not adopted as current legal conclusions merely because the file exists.

**Public read access != blanket permission to copy, modify, redistribute, train, sublicense or commercialize.** Equally, this notice is not a legal mechanism to prohibit independent creation, lawful quotation, accessibility uses, statutory software exceptions, fair use/fair dealing or other rights provided by applicable law.

## 3. Permission request protocol
A request for additional use should specify: requester and contact; precise paths/commit hashes; requested rights (copy, modify, distribute, commercial use, model training, sublicensing); territory, term, attribution, media, derivatives; required licenses of third parties; privacy/security considerations. Only an authorized rights-holder can agree to a grant in a demonstrable form. An unanswered request is **not consent**. No permissions by silence or symbolic token.

No royalty, charitable pledge, universal penalty, percentage donation, or exclusive control is asserted unless supported by a valid separate instrument and relevant governing law.

## 4. Fundamental rights and safeguarding
Participation is voluntary; there is no retaliation for nonparticipation, criticism, departure, another belief or no religion. No collective culpability. No asserted immunity from generally applicable rules, court orders or child-protection obligations. Data minimization, minors' safeguarding, access rights, and legally required reporting are controls, not marketing promises. Respect free expression, research, criticism, press, and access-to-knowledge exceptions where applicable.

## 5. Evidence and reproducibility protocol
Maintain a chain-of-title and provenance register: `asset_id | path | source_ref | exact_commit | author_claim | independent_evidence | upstream_rights | grant | territorial_limit | version | date | exceptions | reviewer | status`.
Cryptographic hashes prove **content identity relative to a known reference**, not historical creation time, legal ownership, authenticity of the person, or scientific truth by themselves. Preserve negative evidence and `TOKEN_VAZIO`. No claim of worldwide enforceability without forum-specific legal review.

## 6. Disputes, remedies, and court process
Do not presume a globally agreed forum or arbitration merely from GitHub access. Forum, governing law, standing, service, limitation periods, evidence admissibility, emergency relief, monetary remedies, appeals and cross-border enforcement depend on actual facts and local law. Do not threaten automatic fines, absolute injunctions, civil/criminal liability, or damages unsupported by law or judgment. Handle takedown/counter-notice and preservation requests with authorized, logged, proportionate human review.

## 7. Separate license-selection decision
Before changing a licensing position on **new future assets**: (i) identify each rights-holder; (ii) enumerate files and existing grants; (iii) run dependency/SBOM and upstream compatibility review; (iv) choose explicit permission model; (v) approve exact plain-language or standard-license text; (vi) obtain authorized sign-off; (vii) release only on dated revision, respecting prior grants. No code or `LICENSE` root change follows automatically from this proposal.
