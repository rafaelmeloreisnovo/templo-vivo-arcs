## I. Research question and method
Can original expressive works and an ethical educational/spiritual project be protected and responsibly published across 24 systems without inventing a global license, immunities, authorial monopolies over ideas, or binding universal precedent? **Framework answer:** legal protection is territorial and fact-specific; Berne is a floor/coordination framework, not a supranational registrar or copyright court.

**Method:** textual law -> temporal validity -> territorial scope -> legal classification -> relevant holding/ratio -> counterauthority -> facts -> procedural gate -> remedy -> evidence. Interpretive doctrine has persuasive value; `súmula vinculante` as such is a Brazilian constitutional institution, NOT a synonym for binding appellate holdings abroad. Case reports need authoritative copies, pinpoint paragraphs/pages, later history and case-fit before filing.

**SOURCE != ARTIFACT != EXECUTION != EVIDENCE != CLAIM; TOKEN_VAZIO != 0.** The matrix is an **issue-spotting research index**, not a verified 24-country legal opinion. Never rely on unverified entries in legal filings.

## II. Treaties and European layer — not substitutes for domestic law
- **Berne Convention**: art. 2 protectable works; art. 5 national treatment / protection independent of formalities and origin-country protection in relevant circumstances; art. 6bis moral rights; art. 9 reproduction; art. 10 quotation; art. 7 duration with qualifications. Berne does not prove an individual's authorship and does not register algorithms, trademarks or patents.
- **TRIPS / WCT where binding:** complementary obligations; verify treaty status, reservations and domestic implementation jurisdiction by jurisdiction; no presumption that every treaty provision creates an independently enforceable private cause of action.
- **EU**: Directive 2009/24/EC art. 1 distinguishes expression of programs from underlying ideas; Directive 2001/29/EC and Directive 2019/790 may affect exceptions and digital uses; national transposition and court decisions matter. The **CJEU** can authoritatively interpret EU law for member states within scope, not for Switzerland, UK, Iceland, Brazil, US or Canada merely by analogy.
- **ECHR/ECtHR**: distinguish Council of Europe human-rights obligations from EU copyright directives and from private claims; Switzerland and Iceland are ECHR contracting parties, neither an EU member. Human-rights constraints frequently require identification of state action or state duties, facts and proportionality.

## IV. Comparative precedent methodology (doctoral rigor; narrow holdings)
1. **Brazil**: Constitutional art.103-A requires 2/3 STF members following repeated constitutional decisions, binds judiciary/admin subject to scope, revision and reclamação. CPC 927 has distinct binding/respect duties; isolate ratio and material facts before invoking. No general rule that every STJ súmula is súmula vinculante. [CF source](https://www2.camara.leg.br/legin/fed/consti/1988/constituicao-1988-5-outubro-1988-322142-normaatualizada-pl.html).
2. **United States**: *Fourth Estate Public Benefit Corp v Wall-Street.com* (2019) distinguishes copyright protection from the §411 litigation prerequisite; *Google LLC v Oracle America* (2021) resolves fair use on its facts and does not decide all software API copyrightability. *Employment Division v Smith* and *Fulton v Philadelphia* concern religious exercise, not universal legal immunity. See US/UK PR #45 (MERGED as of 2026-10-30) for case-specific counterarguments; the archived text's DRAFT wording does not describe the GitHub PR's live state. Independently verify opinions and subsequent history.
3. **UK**: *R (Hodkin) v Registrar General* [2013] UKSC 77 concerns recognition in a specific statutory context; *Shergill v Khaira* [2014] UKSC 33 permits courts to decide objectively ascertainable issues relevant to civil rights without adjudicating theological truth; neither automatically registers this project as a religious entity. [UK judgments in PR #45](https://github.com/rafaelmeloreisnovo/templo-vivo-arcs/pull/45).
4. **EU**: *Infopaq* C-5/08; *Cofemel* C-683/17 and software-specific rulings require exact holdings and applicable directives, not slogans such as 'every code is protected' or 'a name is patented'.
5. **Canada / Australia**: *CCH Canadian v Law Society* (2004), *IceTV v Nine Network* (2009) are legal-method comparators, not decisions that bind courts elsewhere.
6. **France/Germany/Switzerland/Italy/Iceland + 15 others**: selected on-point ratio, legal status and opposing authorities are **TOKEN_VAZIO_UNVERIFIED** in this iteration; do not fabricate a precedent or claim a judicial consensus.

## V. Litigation readiness: procedural gates, legal doctrine and adversarial review
For each contemplated allegation create `CASE_ID | forum | respondent | standing | jurisdiction | choice_of_law | applicable_version | limitation_period | pleading_elements | burden_of_proof | remedies | binding_authorities | contrary_authorities | facts | evidence_chain | privacy/privilege | procedural_rights | counsel | status`.

**Doctrine:** lex loci protectionis/territoriality requires careful country-by-country analysis; nationality/protection-country and cross-border internet acts are not mechanically identical. Separate (a) existence of protectable expression, (b) who owns rights, (c) validity/scope of grants, (d) protected acts, (e) exceptions/defenses, (f) admissible proof, (g) remedies and enforcement. Trademark/patent, confidentiality, privacy and contracts are separate causes of action.

**Falsification gates:**
- F01 original expression vs bare idea/algorithm/data? F02 chain of title evidenced or merely asserted? F03 third-party/license compatibility cleared? F04 preexisting grants preserved?
- F05 treaty party, scope and local implementation verified? F06 controlling court or merely persuasive foreign judgment? F07 exact statute version and judgment holding pinned?
- F08 state action/public authority threshold for constitutional claim met? F09 free speech/criticism/quotation/accessibility rights considered? F10 statutory software reverse-engineering/research exceptions considered?
- F11 claimant, defendant, forum, standing, service, limitation verified? F12 integrity hash confused with authorship/time? F13 religious identity used to allege immunity?
- F14 minors or sensitive data introduced without lawful process? F15 irreversibility, disclosure or main-branch license change without owner/counsel approval? F16 counterargument and appeal/rollback documented?
