### POLICY P01 — Status
This file is a licensing and provenance policy. It does **not** retroactively replace the historical `LICENSE` file and does not relicense third-party material.

### POLICY P02 — Default rule
Unless a file, directory, package, release, or written agreement expressly states a license, no additional permission to copy, modify, redistribute, sublicense, sell, train on, or commercially exploit original material is granted merely because the material is visible in this repository.

This default rule is subject to mandatory rights, statutory exceptions, limitations, and other rules of applicable law.

### POLICY P03 — Scope separation
Licensing must be evaluated per asset class:

- source code;
- documentation and literary text;
- datasets and database structures;
- images/media;
- names, logos and marks;
- research claims, methods and specifications;
- third-party dependencies or incorporated material.

A license applying to one class must not be presumed to apply to another.

### POLICY P04 — Third-party material
Third-party components remain governed by their respective licenses, notices and applicable law. Nothing in this policy attempts to remove or narrow rights granted by third parties.

### POLICY P05 — Contributions
A contributor must have sufficient rights to submit a contribution. Unless a separate contributor agreement or explicit license applies, acceptance of a contribution does not silently transfer ownership of unrelated rights.

Contributions should preserve provenance sufficient to identify source, author/committer metadata, relevant dependency licenses and the commit that introduced the material.

### POLICY P06 — AI-assisted contributions
Where practical, material produced or transformed with an AI agent should preserve:

`human_request -> agent identity -> output/diff -> human adoption decision -> commit/PR`.

No statement about copyrightability, authorship, inventorship or ownership is inferred solely from the presence or absence of an AI agent; those questions depend on facts and applicable law.

### POLICY P07 — Commercial licensing
Commercial permissions, dual licensing, trademark permissions, patent licenses or private access terms require a separate express written grant identifying the assets and rights covered.

### POLICY P08 — Patent rights
Copyright or repository access does not itself grant a patent license unless an applicable license expressly says so. References to inventions, patents or patentable concepts are not representations that a patent exists, is valid, is enforceable, or will be granted.

### POLICY P09 — Research and claims
Access to research materials does not convert hypotheses into validated scientific claims. Reuse must preserve attribution and provenance where legally or contractually required, and must not misrepresent validation status.

### POLICY P10 — Historical preservation
Historical files, commits, tags and receipts used for provenance should be preserved. Corrections should normally be additive and linked to the earlier state rather than silently rewriting evidentiary history.

### POLICY P11 — Future licensing
When a standard open-source or source-available license is selected for a defined software component, that license should be placed at the closest practical scope and referenced from the repository documentation. This policy remains the provenance/governance layer and must not contradict an express component license.

### POLICY P12 — No legal advice
This policy is a repository governance instrument, not legal advice. Jurisdiction-specific enforcement, registration, patent strategy, contractual restrictions and disputes should be reviewed by qualified counsel.
